\packageentry{array}{array -- Mathematical arrays and row spacing}
\partlabel{Template setup}
\begin{referencecode}
\usepackage{uc3m-report}
\end{referencecode}
The template loads \texttt{array} and defines \texttt{\string\rowL}
and \texttt{\string\rowXL} in
\path{styles/graphics/config.tex}. The package supplies
programmable column declarations such as \verb|>{...}c|;
\texttt{array} itself is a mathematical environment.
\partlabel{Routh array with automatic row struts}
The template extends the syntax to
\verb|\begin{array}[c]{columns}[L]| or \texttt{[XL]}.
The first optional argument sets vertical alignment (\texttt{t},
\texttt{c} or \texttt{b}); the option after the column specification
selects a row-height preset. Omit the final option for ordinary syntax.
\begin{referencecode}
\[
\begin{array}{rcc}[XL]
    s^3: & 1 & 2 \\
    s^2: & 3 & K \\
    s^1: & \dfrac{6-K}{3} & 0 \\
    s^0: & K & 0
\end{array}
\]
\end{referencecode}

\[
\begin{array}{rcc}[XL]
    s^3: & 1 & 2 \\
    s^2: & 3 & K \\
    s^1: & \dfrac{6-K}{3} & 0 \\
    s^0: & K & 0
\end{array}
\]

The presets are already defined in the template as follows:
\begin{referencecode}
\newcommand{\rowL}{\vphantom{\dfrac{M}{M}}}
\newcommand{\rowXL}{\vphantom{\dfrac{M^M}{M_M}}}
\end{referencecode}
These invisible struts reserve
height and depth without adding width. They set a minimum row size;
taller entries can still enlarge a row. These presets are template
extensions, not options provided by the \texttt{array} package.

\begin{nobreaks}
\partlabel{Local spacing without the template extension}
Use a column declaration to insert a strut automatically, or add a
custom strut explicitly to each row. This example uses the latter,
as in Homework-3, and adds \texttt{3pt} after the first row.
\begin{referencecode}
\[
\begingroup
\newcommand{\rowstrut}{\vphantom{\dfrac{K^2}{K+1}}}
\begin{array}{rcc}
    s^2: & 1 & K \rowstrut \\[3pt]
    s^1: & \dfrac{K+2}{3} & 0 \rowstrut
\end{array}
\endgroup
\]
\end{referencecode}

\[
\begingroup
\newcommand{\rowstrut}{\vphantom{\dfrac{K^2}{K+1}}}
\begin{array}{rcc}
    s^2: & 1 & K \rowstrut \\[3pt]
    s^1: & \dfrac{K+2}{3} & 0 \rowstrut
\end{array}
\endgroup
\]

Equivalently, \verb|\begin{array}{>{\rowXL}rcc}| applies
the existing preset to every row through the first column.
Use \verb|\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}| within a group to
scale the standard row strut locally. It does not scale the fractions
or guarantee extra clearance around arbitrarily tall entries.
\end{nobreaks}
