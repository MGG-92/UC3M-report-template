\partlabel{Signed values and unavailable results}
Reserve space for a minus sign with \texttt{table-format=-3.2}.
Protect text headers with braces so \texttt{siunitx} does not parse them
as numbers. Use a text placeholder when a result is not defined.
\begin{referencecode}
\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \caption{Measured changes.}
    \begin{tabular}{l S[table-format=-3.2]}
        \toprule
        Test & {Change (\unit{\newton})} \\
        \midrule
        A & -12.50 \\
        B & 105.25 \\
        C & {---} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
\end{referencecode}

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Measured changes.}
    \begin{tabular}{l S[table-format=-3.2]}
        \toprule
        Test & {Change (\unit{\newton})} \\
        \midrule
        A & -12.50 \\
        B & 105.25 \\
        C & {---} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
The placeholder is text, not a zero-valued measurement. The format reserves
column width; it does not round the underlying data. To round only the
display, use \texttt{round-mode=places,round-precision=2} on the
\texttt{S} column, and retain full precision in the calculation program.
