Header and footer shown at reduced page height.
\texttt{geometry} sets the margins; \texttt{fancyhdr} sets the header and
footer. For one page without either, use \verb|\thispagestyle{empty}|.
\texttt{\string\linewidth} is the local width; \texttt{\string\textwidth}
is the full page text width.

\partlabel{Unnumbered heading in the contents}
\begin{referencecode}
\tableofcontents
% An unnumbered section included in the contents:
\section*{Acknowledgements}
\phantomsection
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Acknowledgements}
\end{referencecode}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Contents}\par
Acknowledgements\dotfill 1\par\medskip
\textbf{Acknowledgements}
\end{quote}
The heading stays unnumbered while its entry appears in the contents.
Compile twice to resolve references. Figure and table labels go after
their captions; equation labels go inside the equation environment.

\packageentry{space}{needspace -- Page break control}
\partlabel{Preamble}
\begin{referencecode}
\usepackage{needspace,lipsum}
\end{referencecode}
\partlabel{Reserve room before a heading}
\begin{referencecode}
\lipsum[1][1-3]

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{Results}
\lipsum[2][1-4]
\end{referencecode}

\begingroup
\renewcommand{\subsection}[1]{\subsubsection*{#1}}

\lipsum[1][1-3]

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{Results}
\lipsum[2][1-4]
\par
\endgroup
If fewer than eight baseline spacings remain, the heading and its opening
text start on the next page.
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{53mm}X@{}}\toprule
Command & Use \\\midrule
\texttt{\string\Needspace\{3cm\}} & Break if less than 3 cm remains. \\
\texttt{\string\needspace\{3cm\}} & Approximate reservation. \\
\texttt{\string\Needspace*\{3cm\}} & Preserve bottom alignment with \texttt{\string\flushbottom}. \\
\texttt{\string\newpage} & Force a page break. \\
\texttt{\string\clearpage} & Break and place pending floats first. \\
\bottomrule\end{tabularx}
The requested length must include the heading and its spacing. The command
does not measure the following block or control floating figures.
