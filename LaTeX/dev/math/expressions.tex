\subsection{Amsmath -- Mathematical Expressions}

The \texttt{amsmath} package extends the mathematical capabilities
of \LaTeX, providing additional environments for equations,
alignment, and matrices.


\subsubsection{Equations}

A numbered equation can be created using the \texttt{equation}
environment:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=latex]
\begin{equation}
    F = ma
\end{equation}
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{equation}
    F = ma
\end{equation}

The starred version removes the equation number:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=latex]
\begin{equation*}
    E = mc^2
\end{equation*}
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{equation*}
    E = mc^2
\end{equation*}


\subsubsection{Aligned Equations}

The \texttt{align} environment aligns several equations at a
common point. The alignment point is indicated using \verb|&|:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=latex]
\begin{align}
    F &= ma \\
    a &= \frac{F}{m}
\end{align}
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{align}
    F &= ma \\
    a &= \frac{F}{m}
\end{align}


\subsubsection{Multiple Lines in One Equation}

The \texttt{split} environment can be used when a single equation
must be written across several lines:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=latex]
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
    E &= T + V \\
      &= \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + mgh
\end{split}
\end{equation}
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
    E &= T + V \\
      &= \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + mgh
\end{split}
\end{equation}

All lines share the same equation number.


\subsubsection{Matrices}

Different matrix environments can be used depending on the desired
delimiters:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=latex]
\[
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 & 2 \\
    3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
\]
\end{lstlisting}

\[
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
    1 & 2 \\
    3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
\]

Other common matrix types are:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=latex]
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
    1 & 2 \\
    3 & 4
\end{pmatrix}
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix}
    1 & 2 \\
    3 & 4
\end{vmatrix}
\]
\end{lstlisting}

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
    1 & 2 \\
    3 & 4
\end{pmatrix}
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix}
    1 & 2 \\
    3 & 4
\end{vmatrix}
\]
