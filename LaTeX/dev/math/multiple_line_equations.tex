\subsubsection{Multiple Lines in One Equation}

The \texttt{split} environment can be used inside an
\texttt{equation} when a single equation requires several lines:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=latex]
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
    E &= T + V \\
      &= \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + mgh
\end{split}
\end{equation}
\end{lstlisting}
