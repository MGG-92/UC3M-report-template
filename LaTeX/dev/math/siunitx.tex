Set \texttt{output-decimal-marker=\{,\}} for a decimal comma and
\texttt{per-mode=reciprocal} for negative unit powers.
\texttt{\string\qty} and \texttt{\string\unit} require \texttt{siunitx} v3;
the older v2 equivalents are \texttt{\string\SI} and \texttt{\string\si}.

\partlabel{Signed angles, percentages and time}
\begin{referencecode}
\[
    \phi_{\mathrm{dep}} = \qty{-116.5651}{\degree}
    \qquad \Delta K = \qty{330.3915}{\percent}
    \qquad t_s = \qty{4.2}{\second}
\]
\end{referencecode}

\[
    \phi_{\mathrm{dep}} = \qty{-116.5651}{\degree}
    \qquad \Delta K = \qty{330.3915}{\percent}
    \qquad t_s = \qty{4.2}{\second}
\]

Use \verb|\qty{value}{\degree}| for decimal angles in calculations,
and \verb|\qty{value}{\percent}| for percentages. Supply the sign
in the numeric argument; \texttt{siunitx} formats it with the value.

\partlabel{Control-system margins and angular frequencies}

    \qquad \GM_{\mathrm{dB}} = \qty{6.0206}{\decibel}

\[

\[
    \PM = \qty{21.3886}{\degree}
    \qquad \GM_{\mathrm{dB}} = \qty{6.0206}{\decibel}
\]
\[
    \omega_{gc} = \qty{8.3330e-1}{\radian\per\second}
\]
Use \verb|\decibel| for logarithmic gain values and
\verb|\radian\per\second| for angular frequencies. The numeric
argument accepts scientific notation such as \texttt{8.3330e-1}.
The \verb|\PM| and \verb|\GM| helpers are defined by the template;
see the equations section on page~\pageref{equations}.
